# Q1-v2 independent evaluation preregistration

**Status: BLOCKED_NO_INDEPENDENT_DATA.** No dataset has been acquired or approved. The following outcome rules are frozen before acquisition. Any change requires a separately identified protocol and documented reason while the new test data remain unopened. P0 outputs and archived Q1 Holdout are excluded from any confirmatory sample.

## Source, access and inclusion

Collect synchronized RGB–Thermal originals at new, previously unseen sites and sequences. Document consenting/authorized collection, permitted research use and future sharing terms, sensor model, capture settings and sequence timestamps. Freeze the capture plan and manifest before scoring. Group by physical scene, sequence and close temporal neighbors. Link exact duplicates, near duplicates, augmented derivatives and repeated views before locking group membership. Exclude any overlap with MFNet, Q1 fit/calibration, Q1 Holdout or other historical evaluation images. Assign day/night by recorded capture rather than image content. Require at least **40 independent groups**, **15 day** and **15 night** groups, and **15 groups with positive blur support**. Collect both intact thermal and paired controlled defocus, recording the optical setting and transition independently from the images. Evaluate physically induced defocus separately from digitally synthesized local blur, and do not infer anomaly labels from the quality score or segmentation. Low-texture eligibility uses the pre-frozen Calibration threshold in Q1, whose support and healthy false alarms are reported. If acquisition cannot produce geographically and temporally independent support and explicit labels, the confirmation stays blocked.

The **primary challenge** is independently sampled paired local blur, generated using the precommitted Q1 box size, independently seeded random centers and two fixed blur tiers on the new images. Because blur is wholly absent from all development and model calibration, this challenge measures transfer to an unseen synthetic family on new scenes. Document cameras, day/night and untouched source digests, and retain processed and original versions in the same group. The labels are synthetic transformation regions. Controlled physical defocus is a secondary check with its own instrument labels and assumptions. Do not report this challenge as calibrated detection of natural failures. Report seen occlusion and noise separately using the same frozen Q1 recipe and seeds as secondary sanity checks, without updating anything. Retain identical RGB for paired synthetic views. No model receives a reference image, transformation metadata or target mask as input.

## Endpoints and decision

AUPRC is average precision over 256-level frozen probabilities on all grid points in degraded views. Balanced Brier is the equal-class mean of squared probability errors on the same support. Report positive fraction and chance AP, all-point Brier, operating-point recall, healthy FPR, observability coverage, and stratification by tier, day/night and low texture. Keep all points even when marked unobservable. The primary comparison is Thermal multiscale minus matched RGB-only. Report Q1, spectral ablation, shuffle and prevalence constant as prespecified controls. Key mechanistic corroboration is paired bad-score direction on the altered Thermal ROI relative to the same intact Thermal; RGB score must stay identical. Report full confusion and healthy errors by group. Estimate paired 95% intervals by 10,000 common visual-group resamples with PCG64 seed 3407, recomputing AP and Brier from grouped histograms and ignoring only metric-undefined draws. Report valid draws, and do not treat pixels as independent.

Use the fixed Calibration threshold from P0. Healthy overall FPR must be at most **7%**, day and night separately and low-texture healthy FPR each at most **10%**. Held-out blur recall must be at least **20%**. Require positive median paired bad-probability change on the synthetic ROI, including ties, and exact RGB output identity.

- **PASS** requires adequate independent support, engineering integrity, AUPRC difference lower CI above zero, balanced-Brier difference upper CI below zero, the operational limits and directional check all met.
- **FAIL** with adequate support if a guard fails, the paired bad-score direction fails, or either primary CI is wholly adverse. Any data leakage or repeat use invalidates confirmation.
- **INCONCLUSIVE** applies when group support is insufficient, or when the primary intervals touch zero and the operational and integrity conditions hold. Report negative observations even in an under-supported set. Primary success does not establish downstream segmentation utility.

References to data in this document specify a future collection, with licensing, owners, data-access scope and acquisition lead still unresolved. Do not substitute the existing 127-image Q1 Holdout or the 157-image historical segmentation validation.
