Noncommercial use and adaptation are possible under the license, subject to attribution and applicable share-alike obligations on redistribution. The release documents synchronized modalities, per-sequence calibration and rectified stereo. Pixel registration across RGB and Thermal needs verification. [Project](https://sites.google.com/view/multi-spectral-stereo-dataset/home) · [Format](https://sites.google.com/view/multi-spectral-stereo-dataset/data-format) | The supplement describes 24 raw ROS bags and the sequence page documents day/night and unavailable sequences. [Supplement](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/supplemental/Shin_Deep_Depth_Estimation_CVPR_2023_supplemental.pdf) · [Sequences](https://sites.google.com/view/multi-spectral-stereo-dataset/sequence-info) | BLOCKED pending complete available site/session roster, capture time and day/night receipts, cross-modal alignment and input-format verification. | Teledyne FLIR ADAS v2 | The release lists 7,498 video frames with 1:1 RGB/T correspondence at 24 Hz and separate still collections. Frame pairing does not certify pixel registration. Pre-AGC samples are 16-bit. The full governing download terms covering local audit and derivation were not verified. [Official access](https://oem.flir.com/solutions/automotive/adas-dataset-form/) | Day/night footage from the United States, United Kingdom and France is reported. Countries and frame totals do not establish group counts. Need canonical site/session metadata, near-frame grouping and historical overlap review. [Release](https://oem.flir.com/en-150/about/news/expanded-teledyne-flir-starter-thermal-dataset-for-adas-and-autonomous-vehicle-testing/) | BLOCKED pending applicable terms, paired file and calibration metadata, commissioned grouping and a provenance audit. |
| KAIST Multispectral Pedestrian | The paper documents beam-splitter alignment, paired temporal sequences and day/night acquisition. Data permissions for local derived degradations and auditing were not established from the public distribution page. [Release](https://soonminhwang.github.io/rgbt-ped-detection/data/) · [Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2015/papers/Hwang_Multispectral_Pedestrian_Detection_2015_CVPR_paper.pdf) | The 95,328 pairs are temporally correlated. No verified 40-group, day/night or blur-positive count after grouping. Separate collection provenance does not exclude re-exports or overlap. | BLOCKED pending rights, site/session roster and historical re-export screening. |
| LLVIP | The official release documents 15,488 aligned visible–infrared pairs. Its terms stipulate noncommercial use and attribution and reserve rights. Obtain explicit clarification for local derivatives/audit if reconsidered as a contributor. [Release](https://bupt-ai-cz.github.io/LLVIP/) · [Terms](https://github.com/bupt-ai-cz/LLVIP/blob/main/Term%20of%20Use%20and%20License.md) | The public roster has 26 scenes, primarily dark, with two daytime scenes. Dark imagery does not certify time of capture. No historical overlap proof. | Insufficient as a standalone source. |
| MSRS | The authors explicitly derived MSRS from MFNet and enhanced the retained pairs. A source-code license would not establish independent image rights. [Author repository](https://github.com/Linfeng-Tang/MSRS/blob/main/README.md) | Known overlap through derivation even when byte hashes differ. | Excluded. |
| New authorized synchronized collection | Obtain a written research, local derivation and local audit grant. Log synchronized RGB/T exposure times, calibration, capture settings, camera ID and authorized collection location. | Plan at least 40 independent physical groups after deduplication, at least 15 in each lighting stratum and at least 15 groups that can support the frozen digital blur. Acquire separate paired optical-defocus captures with an instrument log, not a model-derived label. | Proposed acquisition route. Permission, roster and images are pending. |

All public candidates lack a verified ledger of intact frames, independent groups and eligible positive-blur support. None has documented instrument-labelled physical defocus suitable for the frozen secondary check. A different dataset name or sensor does not prove independence. Before any score is generated, document pixel registration, the P0 input representation and a fixed sampling grid. Do not choose quantization, AGC, alignment or resampling by inspecting model outputs. If compatible originals or an admissible input mapping cannot be established under the existing protocol, retain blocked status.
