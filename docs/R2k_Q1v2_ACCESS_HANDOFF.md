# Q1-v2 external RGB-T access handoff

## Frozen input contract

P0 uses decoded 8-bit grayscale values on the 640 × 480 image plane for Thermal and the RGB-only control. A provider’s native 8-bit three-channel visible image may use the unchanged OpenCV grayscale decoder. The matching native Thermal image must be single-channel uint8. Both modalities must represent the same capture on the same pixel grid. Temporal synchronization, intrinsics/extrinsics or equal dimensions alone do not certify that alignment. The original score and target grid is 80 × 60, with zero-based coordinates 4 + 8k along each axis. The descriptors use scales 3, 9 and 21. Calibration, probability bins, observable flags and thresholds stay frozen. The local blur box is 96 × 96 with a 12-pixel margin. Changing pixel scale would change the physical meaning of the descriptor scales and blur. [P0 protocol](../configs/R2k_Q1v2_protocol.json) · [Independent evaluation](R2k_Q1v2_INDEPENDENT_EVALUATION.md)

Approve a direct decode only for a documented provider-issued, registered native release already on that grid. Check byte hashes and source representation before grayscale decoding. Cropping, resizing, geometric warping, requantization, custom AGC or contrast remapping to manufacture compliant inputs are `PROTOCOL_CHANGE_REQUIRED`. Before access or scoring under a revised protocol, specify a single mapping per device with fixed response range, clipping, calibration, registration and interpolation, crop and valid support rules. Calibrate those mappings on independent instrument targets or approved development material and freeze the software and checksums. Never select mappings or parameters using candidate quality scores.

## MS²

- The public sequence format includes synchronized and stereo-rectified RGB/NIR/Thermal files with timestamps, calibration and GPS/IMU. It also describes depth and odometry products. The published supplement lists 24 raw ROS bags. The authors withhold suburban sequences, so the paper inventory is not an entitlement to every bag. Request a versioned, actually distributable inventory, `sync_data` with RGB/T and calibration, and timezone, session/route, overlapping route and lighting metadata. [Format](https://sites.google.com/view/multi-spectral-stereo-dataset/data-format) · [Sequence availability](https://sites.google.com/view/multi-spectral-stereo-dataset/sequence-info) · [Supplement](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/supplemental/Shin_Deep_Depth_Estimation_CVPR_2023_supplemental.pdf)
- The CVPR release used synchronized 15 fps RGB/NIR/Thermal capture. The RGB sensor is 2448 × 2048 and thermal A65C is 640 × 512 with uint16 raw sensor values. Rectified and cropped files in the supplement are RGB 1224 × 384 and Thermal 640 × 256. Each stereo pair has its own camera plane. Calibration includes intrinsics, extrinsics and left-NIR-to-sensor transforms. Cross-modal common-grid registration is unverified. Raw counts are not automatically temperatures. [Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/papers/Shin_Deep_Depth_Estimation_From_Thermal_Image_CVPR_2023_paper.pdf) · [Supplement](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/supplemental/Shin_Deep_Depth_Estimation_CVPR_2023_supplemental.pdf) · [Format](https://sites.google.com/view/multi-spectral-stereo-dataset/data-format)
- Publicly documented representations require `PROTOCOL_CHANGE_REQUIRED` unless a native compatible release is established. Mapping raw counts to 8 bits can clip or flatten thermal contrast. Registration and resizing may suppress edge energy, crop support or inject interpolation artifacts. Under the current conservative sequence rule, the 24 documented bags alone cannot yield 40 independent groups. Repeated routes reduce effective support further. Additional genuinely independent sessions would need verified availability and daytime/nighttime support. [Supplement](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/supplemental/Shin_Deep_Depth_Estimation_CVPR_2023_supplemental.pdf)
- The project lists CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, with attribution and share-alike terms for redistribution of adaptations. For a noncommercial project, have an authorized user review the applicable release and submit the official download survey. Prepare name, institutional affiliation, contact email and intended noncommercial research, derivation and local audit scope. These are preparation items. Exact survey fields were not verified. The official page describes an automated link response and lists an author contact for download issues. [License and citation](https://sites.google.com/view/multi-spectral-stereo-dataset/home) · [Official request](https://sites.google.com/view/multi-spectral-stereo-dataset/download)

## Teledyne FLIR ADAS v2

- Request the paired video archive first. The provider reports 7,498 video frames with 1:1 visible and Thermal correspondence at 24 Hz. The 9,711 Thermal and 9,233 RGB stills have separate counts and cannot be assumed paired. The format table distinguishes 14-bit no-AGC TIFF (the overview also calls out 16-bit pre-AGC frames) from 8-bit AGC Thermal JPEG and 8-bit RGB JPEG. Obtain the archive documentation to verify storage versus effective bit depth and the available representation for each paired frame. [Official specifications and form](https://oem.flir.com/solutions/automotive/adas-dataset-form/)
- The documented Tau 2 Thermal sensor is 640 × 512. The RGB camera is Blackfly S BFS-U3-51S5C with a different field of view. The provider page does not specify the released paired visible dimensions, pixel-to-pixel mapping, synchronization hardware or tolerance. Obtain the actual pair map, archive dimensions, calibration and intrinsics/extrinsics, timestamp basis, capture locations/routes and repeated-session and lighting labels. The published collection spans day and night, but the independent group counts and daylight quotas are unverified. [Specifications](https://oem.flir.com/solutions/automotive/adas-dataset-form/) · [Release](https://oem.flir.com/en-150/about/news/expanded-teledyne-flir-starter-thermal-dataset-for-adas-and-autonomous-vehicle-testing/)
- The 8-bit option eliminates a custom bit-depth reduction only. AGC can change the intensity scale between frames and JPEG can introduce edge artifacts. If the released images require crop, registration or resizing, mark `PROTOCOL_CHANGE_REQUIRED`. A native provider release still needs verified 640 × 480 grids, alignment and a documented intensity representation. No compatible archive was verified.
- Begin at the official download form. The form displays first/last name, email, company and country/state selectors. Use truthful institutional information. Review the privacy conditions and obtain the **dataset-use terms** governing the booked version. Free access and a privacy notice do not establish permission for local derived degradations or independent audits. If the terms do not cover those uses, request a written grant from the provider before transfer. [Official access form](https://oem.flir.com/solutions/automotive/adas-dataset-form/)

## Request text and private handoff

> I am requesting the paired RGB and thermal release and associated capture metadata for an internal CPU-only research evaluation of thermal image quality. Please confirm whether local synthetic blur derivation, file and near-duplicate auditing, and publication of aggregate results are permitted. We will keep the original media and private metadata local. Please provide a versioned paired inventory, available camera calibration, the thermal bit-depth and AGC settings, capture timestamps with timezone, and route/site/session and lighting records sufficient to identify independent groups and repeated routes.

Submit via the official channel yourself, after confirming your authority and the applicable terms. Provide Codex only the absolute path to the private local archive, a local copy of the applicable terms and scope of the grant, confirmed version and checksums, and a path to the metadata and calibration. Keep authorization correspondence, personal data, live download URLs and source files outside Git.

First compare original digests with the frozen MFNet digest manifest, including the historical Q1 partitions, plus allowed inventories of other historical assets. No official test pixels are needed for this comparison. An authorized independent reviewer should also compare licensed imagery and available provenance for flips, crops, re-encodings, online re-exports, repeated routes and near frames, documenting any unresolved comparison coverage. Keep related frames and derivatives in a single group and archive exclusion reasons. Do not recover group counts by splitting an adjacent video. A combined-source study requires prior source strata, per-sensor representation and grouping rules, a cross-source duplicate review and a plan to report device shift. Retain the minimum 40 groups, 15 day, 15 night and 15 positive-blur groups after exclusion. If metadata or permission is unavailable, remain blocked or arrange a newly authorized synchronized capture.
